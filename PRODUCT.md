## Users

Two audiences weighted equally (confirmed):

- Guests: couples, families, and small groups, initially from Pampanga and nearby areas, planning a private getaway and deciding whether to message the host. This is a proposed design audience, not verified demographic data.
- Instructor: grades the mockup as a school assignment (single-page informational website with in-page navigation, responsive layouts, prototype states, print reference).

## Product Purpose

The Quvoh is a private, kubo-style accommodation in Arayat, Pampanga. The single-page website explains the stay, shows the real property, states rates and rules plainly, and routes visitors to Facebook Messenger or Instagram to inquire. Success: a visitor understands what they get, what it costs, how many guests fit, and how to ask, within seconds, and the file satisfies the assignment.

## Positioning

The whole place is yours: one raised bamboo kubo with its own pool, veranda, kitchen, and outdoor bathroom, booked as a single private stay rather than rooms in a resort.

## Operating Context

- Inquiries happen in Facebook Messenger and Instagram DMs. A message is not a booking.
- Guests arrive at 2:00 p.m. and leave at 12:00 noon the next day (22-hour stay).
- The owner confirms availability and reservations directly.

- Maximum 8 guests. Parking included (number of spaces unconfirmed).
- Poolan game table; speaker with microphones.
- Complimentary toothbrush, toothpaste, soap, shampoo, and coffee.

- Check-in 2:00 p.m.; check-out 12:00 noon the following day.
- Public location: Arayat, Pampanga.

Undecided / pending (never present as fact):

## Product Principles

1. Truth over polish: every price, limit, and amenity shown is supplied by the client; unknowns stay in internal notes.
2. The real place leads: actual photographs carry the page, uncropped where cropping would misrepresent the property.
3. Costs are never fine print: base rate, the 4-guest inclusion, extra-guest fee, and the 8-guest cap are equally visible.
4. A message is not a booking: every inquiry path says so.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design targets (not an audit): readable labels and errors, visible keyboard focus, roughly 44 px minimum targets, AA contrast, no hover-only information, captions and transcript requirements recorded for video, alt-text suggestions for informative images.
